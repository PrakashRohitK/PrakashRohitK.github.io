Do LLMs Lie More as They Get Bigger?
Spoiler: alignment matters more than parameters.
AI / NLP
PyTorch · Pythia (70M–2.8B) · Qwen2.5 · SQuAD 2.0 · FEVER
  • Benchmarked the full Pythia scale suite on factual reliability, exposing the Abstention Gap — base models score ~0% on "I don't know" regardless of how many parameters they have. Bigger is not safer.
  • Caught Prompt-Induced Bias Flips on FEVER: few-shot prompting nudged larger models toward a systematic REFUTES bias, proving truthfulness is an alignment property — not a free gift from scale.
  • Showed scale improves open-domain recall log-linearly, but does not improve factual precision of retrieval. The model remembers more; it doesn't know more.
